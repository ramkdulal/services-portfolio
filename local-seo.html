Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding local search strategy and Google Business Profile execution.

How does Local SEO help my business appear in Google's Map Pack? +

Google determines Local Map Pack rankings based on relevance, distance, and prominence. Local SEO optimizes your Google Business Profile, standardises NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data across citations, and builds local contextual signals to maximise visibility in local search queries.

Why is Local Schema markup critical for GEO and Voice Search? +

LocalBusiness schema provides explicit structural metadata about your geographic location, opening hours, service radius, and offerings. AI models and voice assistants rely on this precise data to answer queries like "Find a specialist near me" accurately.

How do you manage SEO for businesses with multiple locations? +

Multi-location SEO requires dedicated, non-duplicate location pages for each physical site or service area. Each page is engineered with localized schema, location-specific content, embeddable maps, and tailored review integration.
