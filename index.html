Frequently Asked Questions

Key insights on modern search strategies and methodologies.

What is Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)? +

Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) is the strategy of structuring web content so that AI generative engines (such as Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and ChatGPT) easily extract, credit, and cite your content within synthesized answer summaries.

How does Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) differ from traditional SEO? +

While traditional SEO focuses on driving clicks from ranked listings on search results pages, AEO aims to directly satisfy natural language queries through structured data, precise entity definitions, and succinct answer blocks ideal for AI assistants and voice search.

Why is Technical SEO essential for web growth? +

Without a solid technical foundation, search engine bots struggle to crawl, render, and index your pages effectively. Optimising JavaScript rendering, Core Web Vitals, site speed, and architectural hierarchy guarantees maximum search visibility.
