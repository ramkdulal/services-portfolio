E-Commerce SEO & GEO

185% Organic Revenue Scale for Enterprise E-Commerce Store

Challenge: Severe index bloating and duplicate content issues caused by unmanaged faceted navigation across 25,000+ SKU URLs.

Solution: Redesigned taxonomy routing, deployed strict canonical rules, optimized product JSON-LD feeds, and structured product attributes for AI shopping engine discovery.

Result: +185% increase in organic revenue and a 42% improvement in crawl efficiency within 6 months.
Technical SEO & Performance

Core Web Vitals Remediation & JS SEO Overhaul

Challenge: Client-side rendered React application suffering from poor indexation and failing Core Web Vitals (INP and LCP).

Solution: Transitioned critical rendering paths to Server-Side Rendering (SSR), deferred non-critical JavaScript execution, and optimized image compression pipelines.

Result: Passed 100% of Core Web Vitals URLs and achieved a +120% rise in indexed pages.
Local SEO & AEO

Result: Top 3 Map Pack rankings secured for 85% of primary target locations, driving a +310% increase in direct calls.
