Data-Driven Optimisation for Online Stores

E-Commerce websites present unique SEO challenges that standard content-led strategies cannot fix. Managing thousands of product URLs, dynamic stock levels, faceted filtering, and canonical parameter traps requires technical precision.

My E-Commerce SEO services focus on scalable solutions: structuring category taxonomies, optimizing crawl budgets, deploying rich product schema for Google Merchant Center, and configuring store data so conversational AI tools can discover and recommend your products.

The E-Commerce Optimisation Pillars

Four technical and strategic areas engineered to drive sustainable sales growth.

1. Faceted Navigation & Crawl Budget

Prevent index bloating and crawl waste. I design canonical and indexing rules for filters, search results pages, and multi-attribute products to maximize search engine rendering capacity.

2. Category & Taxonomy Architecture

Organise store hierarchies around high-intent search behaviour. Optimise category hubs, internal link structures, and breadcrumbs to pass page authority down to high-converting product pages.

3. Structured Data & Merchant Feeds

Deploy comprehensive Product, Offer, and AggregateRating schema. Align your on-page technical data with Merchant Center feeds to secure rich snippets and shopping SERP features.

4. GEO & Conversational AI Visibility

Optimise store entity data and product specifications for AI synthesis platforms (ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews), ensuring your brand is recommended during direct answer discovery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about enterprise and mid-market E-Commerce SEO strategies.

How do you handle duplicate content issues caused by faceted navigation? +

Faceted navigation is managed using precise canonicalisation, robots directive tuning, parameter handling in Google Search Console, or AJAX-based rendering to prevent crawl waste and canonical dilution across thousands of filter variations.

What structured data is essential for E-Commerce websites? +

Essential schema markup includes Product, Offer, AggregateRating, Review, MerchantReturnPolicy, and ShippingDetails structured data. This helps Google display rich snippets and list products accurately in Google Shopping and AI answer engines.

How does GEO impact E-Commerce search visibility? +

Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) ensures your product data and store authority are properly indexed by AI platforms like Perplexity and ChatGPT, allowing conversational search engines to cite and recommend your store during natural language product queries.
