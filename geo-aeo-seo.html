Frequently Asked Questions

Key insights on AI discovery, prompt optimization, and answer engine visibility.

What is Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)? +

Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) focuses on structuring brand data and content so that conversational AI models (such as Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Google AI Overviews) extract, credit, and cite your site as an authoritative source in AI-generated answers.

How does Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) differ from traditional SEO? +

While traditional SEO focuses on ranking web pages in organic search results, AEO formats information into concise, structured, direct answers to capture featured snippets, voice assistant responses, and conversational search placements.

How do you measure visibility in AI search engines? +

AI visibility is measured by tracking citation frequency, source attribution rates, entity presence within Knowledge Graphs, and brand inclusion across prompt-based test matrices in generative engines.
